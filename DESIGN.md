# LWNTL interface

Desktop translation workspace for long reading and editing sessions. Material-inspired surfaces and restrained elevation, implemented with shadcn/ui and Radix primitives.

Palette: page #f6f8fc, surface #ffffff, text #172033, muted text #58677f, border #dbe2ec, primary #245cce. Dark mode uses cool slate surfaces with brighter blue and semantic status tones.

Typography: Segoe UI with system fallbacks for offline Windows use; a readable serif for translated prose. Sentence case, medium-weight labels, and headings that establish the content hierarchy.

Layout: compact application bar; left-aligned content; a spacious collection view; dense, scrollable settings and editor panels. The source/translation pair remains the identity of the workspace. No marketing content or new imagery.

Radii: 8px controls, 12px panels, 16px dialogs. Thin borders separate content; shadows distinguish floating menus and dialogs. Blue marks the primary action and active state. Existing group colors and status tones retain their meaning.

Lucide icons remain for recognizable editing, navigation, document and language actions, matching shadcn controls. No decorative icons or generated assets.

Dial: ENERGY 1 / RHYTHM 2 / MOTION 1. Motion is limited to short feedback on user actions. Reduced motion is respected.
